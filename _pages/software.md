## CHIDES
{: .archive__subtitle}

Charles Hermite Interpolation Differential Equation Solver --- a framework for
Hermite methods for hyperbolic and hyperbolic-parabolic systems.

**Not yet publicly released.** [Contact me](mailto:appelo@vt.edu) if you would
like access.

## dg_dath_elastic
{: .archive__subtitle}

An energy based discontinuous Galerkin solver for the elastic wave equation in
second order form.

[bitbucket.org/appelo/dg_dath_elastic_v1.0](https://bitbucket.org/appelo/dg_dath_elastic_v1.0)

## PeWe
{: .archive__subtitle}

Particular solutions to the elastic wave equation in curved geometries, for use
as manufactured solutions when verifying elastic wave solvers.

[bitbucket.org/appelo/pewe](https://bitbucket.org/appelo/pewe)
